Common questions

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days free. You won't be charged until the trial ends, then it's $6.99 per month. Cancel anytime at least 24 hours before it renews.

How do I cancel?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Horizon. On Mac: App Store → your name → Account Settings → Subscriptions. You can also tap Manage subscription in Horizon's Settings.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open Horizon's Settings and tap Restore purchases (also on the subscription screen). Sign in with the same Apple Account.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything is stored in your own iCloud. The developer can't see it. See the Privacy Policy.

My data isn't syncing between devices

Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on for Horizon, and that you have a connection. Syncing can take a minute.

How do I share with my partner?

Use the share option in Horizon's Settings to invite them by iCloud. Private notes stay private.
