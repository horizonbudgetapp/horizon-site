Horizon Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Horizon is a budgeting, planning and savings app. Your privacy is built into how it works: we don't have accounts, we don't run servers that hold your data, and we don't collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer of Horizon does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Where your information lives

Everything you enter (budget items, amounts, goals, notes, moods) is stored on your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. That data belongs to you and is protected by your Apple Account. We cannot see it.

Partner sharing

If you choose to share your plan with a partner, Apple's CloudKit sharing sends it only to the person you invite. Private notes stay in your own iCloud and are not shared. You can stop sharing at any time.

No bank connections

Horizon never connects to your bank or financial accounts. You enter everything yourself.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings.

Notifications and calendar

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. If you choose to add bills to Apple Calendar, that happens on your device with your permission.

Children

Horizon is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
